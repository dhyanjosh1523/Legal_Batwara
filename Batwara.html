About impartible assets

Tick "Impartible" for assets that cannot be split: a family deity or temple, records, rare books, unique jewellery, art, historic weapons, a small house, a business, religious property, or anything that loses much value if split. They are never divided physically. Their value still counts, and the estimate shows who is compensated.
